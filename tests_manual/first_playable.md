# First playable smoke checklist

- [ ] Main scene opens; player can walk around without passing through room geometry.
- [ ] Desktop: WASD/arrows move, mouse looks, E interacts, Esc releases mouse.
- [ ] Android landscape: left side moves, right side looks, action button interacts.
- [ ] Look at a flower within range; prompt names its flower type; interact picks it up.
- [ ] Inventory stops at five flowers and shows a full-hands notice.
- [ ] Correct basket accepts one matching flower; other flower types stay in inventory.
- [ ] Progress increments once per successful placement.
- [ ] Close and reopen; inventory and placed flowers remain saved.
- [ ] Place all 30 flowers; completion notice appears.
