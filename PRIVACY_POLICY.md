# Privacy Policy — Little Bloom

**Effective date:** set this date when the first public release is submitted.

Little Bloom is an offline flower-sorting game. In the current release design, the app does not ask you to create an account and does not include advertising, analytics, or an external data-collection service.

## Information and storage

The game stores level progress and your control/audio preferences locally on your device so you can continue playing. The app does not send this save or settings data to the developer or another service. Android automatic app-data backup is disabled in the release preset.

## Sharing and third parties

The current project does not request internet access and includes no third-party analytics, advertising, or social SDK. The game uses the Godot Engine to run. Re-check the final Android manifest and bundled libraries before publishing; update this policy and the Play Data safety answers if any network, SDK, or data-handling feature is added.

## Retention and deletion

Local progress remains on the device while the app data remains installed. You can erase it with the in-game “Xóa tiến độ” action, by clearing Little Bloom's app storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app. The developer does not receive a copy of the local save.

## Contact

For privacy questions, use the support contact shown on Little Bloom's Google Play listing. The developer must add a monitored support address to that listing before publication.
